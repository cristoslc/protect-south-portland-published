Writing a letter to the editor

Sentry

Email the Editor; see the Sentry’s website for the submissions address. Letters should be no longer than 350 words and must include the writer’s name, full address, and phone number. The deadline is Monday at noon.

Forecaster

Email the Editor; see the Forecaster’s website for the submissions address. Letters should be no longer than 300 words and must include the writer’s name, full address, and daytime and evening phone numbers. The deadline is Monday at noon.

Portland Press Herald

Email the Editor, or send mail to Editor, Portland Press Herald, P.O. Box 1460, Portland, ME 04104-5009. Letters should be no longer than 300 words and include the writer’s name, address, and daytime phone number.

More ways to help

Follow our alerts to Act now and attend City Council meetings and workshops. You show your support for our causes just by being there, and speaking up if you feel comfortable. You can speak for 2 minutes at a workshop and 3 minutes at the public discussion part of a city council meeting. Special topics at council meetings may only allow 2 minutes.

We need help with small tasks such as phone calling, handouts and computer work. Contact us to volunteer.
