Our work

Protect South Portland’s focus is returning to the tanks and the emissions: what residents at the fence line have felt and smelled for years, and what the new fence-line data now confirms.

South Portland Tank Farms

120 large petroleum tanks run by six major companies. Fence-line data confirms the air exceeds state standards for benzene. See the interactive map, the fence-line readings and the Oct. 27 City Council workshop, all on one page.

Coastal flooding

Storm surge floods our streets and working waterfront. PSP works with the City’s coastal resilience efforts and tracks what flooding means for the tank farms.
